# Joker economy for the GBA build

The GBA shop uses a deliberately different Joker price curve from desktop
Balatro. The shop is smaller, runs are shorter, Planets are applied immediately,
and Alchemical Cards cost `$4-$6`. A permanent Joker should therefore not be the
automatic cheapest and strongest purchase.

## Base-price bands

| Rarity | Base price | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Common | `$4-$7` | Early build pieces and conditional additive scoring |
| Uncommon | `$7-$9` | Retriggers, xMult, rule changes, and stronger engines |
| Rare | `$10-$12` | Build-defining xMult and Joker-copy effects |

The three `$4` entry cards are Joker, Jolly Joker, and Sly Joker. This keeps a
real purchase available with the normal `$4` starting bankroll. Most common
Jokers cost `$5-$6`; Blue Joker and the strongest common economy engine reach
`$7`.

Strong synergy pieces are priced above their old desktop-derived values:

- Hanging Chad: `$6`
- Sock and Buskin: `$8`
- Fibonacci and Joker Stencil: `$9`
- The Duo, Trio, Family, Order, and Tribe: `$10`
- Blueprint and Brainstorm: `$12`

Edition premiums remain additive after the base price:

| Edition | Premium |
| --- | ---: |
| Foil | `+$2` |
| Holographic | `+$3` |
| Polychrome | `+$5` |
| Negative | `+$5` |

Shop discount Vouchers still apply after the edition premium and round the final
price up. Selling a Joker returns half of its base-plus-edition value, rounded
down; a shop discount does not also reduce its later resale value. This preserves
the existing Voucher and resale rules while making the initial permanent
investment meaningful.

## Economy targets

- A normal run starts with `$4`, enough for an entry Common but not every offer.
- One regular Blind reward should open most Common choices without making an
  Uncommon automatic.
- A `$6` Planet competes directly with a mid-tier Common: permanent hand scaling
  versus a new passive effect.
- `$4-$6` Alchemicals remain affordable tactical purchases rather than being
  more expensive than nearly every permanent Joker.
- `$10` Vouchers and Rares require saving, economy effects, or a discount.

These values are intentionally conservative. They should be revisited only from
multi-Ante play data (win rate, average cash before each shop, and purchase rate),
not by lowering isolated offers that feel unaffordable in one shop.
